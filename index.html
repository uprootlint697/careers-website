How we hire

Our interview process

Four steps to explore the role, show your work, and meet the people you’ll work with.

  1. 1
    💬

    Initial Screening Interview

    A short call about the role, the process, and mutual fit.

    ⏱ 15 min
  2. 2
    📋

    Portfolio Showcase

    A short Loom or assignment showcasing your skills and best work.

    ⏱ 5 min
  3. 3
    🧑‍💻

    Team Manager Interview

    Meet your manager to discuss the role’s responsibilities and how you’d work together.

    ⏱ 45 min
  4. 4
    👥

    Executive Interview

    A final conversation with the VP and CEO to assess overall fit and growth potential.

    ⏱ 45 min
📅 Total time: 1–2 weeks

Honest fit check

Before you scroll to the roles.

A quick check on how we work, so you can decide whether it suits you.

You’ll do well here if…

  • You’d rather be handed a target than a process.
  • You can explain what you did and what changed.
  • You write well — most decisions happen in writing.
  • You’re comfortable finding a way forward with limited resources.
  • You take initiative and adjust as you learn.

This probably isn’t for you if…

  • You want a large team to delegate to on day one.
  • You’d rather set strategy than execute it yourself.
  • You need in-person energy to do your best work.
  • Priorities shifting mid-quarter would frustrate you.
  • You prefer an interview process without a portfolio showcase.
